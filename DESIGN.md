# THE BRIDGE — reference-led redesign

White paper, navy ink, cornflower blue display text and pale cyan bridge arcs. A self-hosted Cormorant Garamond display face and Manrope interface face carry the Latin text, with native Chinese serif/sans fallbacks. The reference poster governs the hero composition. No remote font runtime dependency.

Home mode: Persuade. Student forms and staff workspace mode: Operate. Announcements mode: Read. The bridge moves from information (Signal), through private dialogue (Voice), into collective choice (Common Ground).

Inventory: SSES high school; Student Union; THE BRIDGE name; the arc above BRIDGE; paired English/Chinese slogans; three named campus spaces; private receipt code; continued correspondence; polls with deadlines; a student/staff role boundary.

Argument and copy deck:
- Hero: a space to express and a campus to shape — school, platform name, paired slogans and an Explore campus spaces action.
- Signal: keep in the loop — announcements, attachments and campus arrangements; open the read-only feed.
- Voice: your thoughts matter — choose a category, send a letter, keep one code, continue the conversation.
- Common Ground: shape it together — choose within a published limit; follow a deadline; see results after close.
- Footer: the school and platform name return; staff access is a plain, clear link.

Idea: On this page, THE BRIDGE's arc becomes the connecting line between reading, expressing and participating. The live element is the real API-backed campus activity status, with honest unavailable/empty states.

Taste direction: type-only-statement, seed 1490698022. User reference takes precedence over the dealt saturated hero, four-picture floor and footer contact layout: no invented campus photography or ornamental screenshots. The typography, two-ink headline and arc preserve the supplied composition. No unrelated stock images. The brand lockup and requested uppercase school/platform copy are explicit exceptions to the small-label and eyebrow rules. Bridge paths use the reference's cyan/blue/lilac, including its logo gradient; UI surfaces remain flat.

Interaction: 44px targets, visible keyboard focus, 16px main body, clear disabled/loading/error/empty states; mobile layout stacks naturally. Motion is a short headline entrance, restrained bridge drift and section reveals; reduced motion renders the final static composition. Nothing is hidden without JavaScript.

Initial review: dark incumbent conflicts with the white reference; 12px running text and repeated icon cards weaken hierarchy. Initial inspector: 12 FAIL / 4 WARN, recorded in .taste-skill. Rebuild the hero, replace repeated cards with three different editorial chapter compositions, unify the functional surfaces.

1.4.0: Shared footer adds a two-column FAQ and compact release history, stacked on mobile. Native details/summary preserve keyboard interaction. Destructive staff actions name the deleted object and confirm before request; the thread shows its first-reply retention deadline.
